What we do

About us

A family team from the west of Scotland.

Polestar Property Solutions is run by Bernie and Jade. We set Polestar up to do property properly: fair offers for sellers, well-finished homes for tenants, and honest, transparent partnerships with our investors.

Bernie started his working life in the Royal Electrical and Mechanical Engineers (REME), moved into mechanical engineering in the oil and gas sector, and now works as a commercial saturation diver in the North Sea (UK & Norway). He brings his pragmatic, plan-it-right approach to every project.

Jade is a Gas Safe registered engineer with first-hand knowledge of what makes a home safe, compliant, warm and efficient, which shapes how we plan every potential project or refurbishment.

Privacy notice

How we look after your details

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

Polestar Property Solutions Ltd (company no. SC889949, registered office 71 Kilbowie Road, Clydebank, G81 1BL) is the data controller for the personal information you give us. You can contact us about anything in this notice at polestar.property.team@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you use our contact form or email us, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, the postcode of a property, and anything you write in your message.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and keep in touch about it.
  • If you're selling, to assess your property and, if appropriate, make you an offer.
  • If you're interested in investing, to discuss possible deals and partnerships with you.

We use your details because you've asked us to contact you (your consent), because it's needed to take steps towards an agreement you're considering with us, or because we have a legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries. We will not send you marketing unless you ask us to.

Who we share it with

We never sell your details. We only share them where it's needed to progress something you've asked us about, for example with a solicitor, surveyor or lender during a property purchase, and we'll tell you before we do. Our email is provided by Google, and messages sent through our website forms are handled by Formspree, our form service, before reaching us. Both may store data outside the UK under recognised legal safeguards.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry doesn't lead to a deal, we delete your details within 12 months of our last contact. If it does, we keep the records we need for legal, tax and accounting purposes, usually for up to 6 years.

Your rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, to limit how we use it, or to object to us using it. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Just email us and we'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
