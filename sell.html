Before you sell to anyone

Five questions to ask any quick-sale buyer.

  1. Are you the actual buyer?Some firms are middlemen who pass your details on to someone else.
  2. How did you work out your offer?A fair buyer can show you the figures, not just a number.
  3. Could the price drop before the sale is agreed?Watch out for an offer that falls at the last minute. Ask what would change it, and why.
  4. Do I have to pay anything up front?You shouldn't be asked for a fee just to receive an offer.
  5. Can I use my own solicitor?You should always be free to choose your own legal adviser.

Ask us all five. We'll answer every one in writing.

Questions

What sellers usually ask us.

How much will you offer?

We look at what the property would be worth once any work is done, then take off the cost of the work, the buying and selling costs, and a margin for the risk we take on. That's why our offer is usually below full market value. We'll show you the figures so nothing is a mystery.

How quickly can the sale go through?

It mainly depends on the solicitors and the property's title deeds. Straightforward sales can often complete in a few weeks. If you need longer, that's fine too.

Do I need a Home Report?

It depends on how the property is sold. We'll talk you through what's needed in your situation, and your solicitor can confirm it.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. In Scotland a sale only becomes legally binding when the missives are concluded between the two solicitors. Until then you're free to walk away.

Will you buy a property with tenants in it?

Often, yes. Tell us about the tenancy when you get in touch and we'll explain how it would work.

Is my enquiry confidential?

Yes. We only use your details to respond to you and never sell or share them. Our privacy notice on the main Polestar page explains how we handle your information.
